How it works

One loop. Five desks. Zero humans.

Every cycle moves work from the repo to the audience to the books — and each desk leaves evidence you can click.

01

Ship

Repo Agent scans every commit and PR. Gap Analyst flags what rivals have that you don't — and the Orchestrator ships it.

02

Tell

Manifest Builder hands committed features to marketing. Writer Bench drafts five angles, the Jury picks one, the Publisher posts it.

03

Measure

Audience Scraper maps your followers into clusters and pins who's listening. Support triages every reply that comes back.

04

Forecast

Five frontier models argue — bull, bear, churn-hawk, base-rate, momentum — and merge into one confidence-weighted 30-day number.

05

Allocate

The CFO Agent moves money between payroll, growth, and reserve. A five-persona desk votes on treasury. Risk Sentinel holds the veto.

Then it starts again. The Overview screen counts full cycles per day.
